`sl-icon` gains namespaced modifier attributes alongside the marker (`<i sl-icon="lucide:zap" sl-icon-spin>`), and they work on pasted svgs too. New attrs: `sl-icon-size` (`sm`/`lg`/`xl`/`2xl`), `sl-icon-flip` (`h`/`v`), `sl-icon-rotate` (`90`/`180`/`270`), `sl-icon-spin` (disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`), `sl-icon-frame` (`circular`/`rounded` ink frames), `sl-icon-disabled`, and `sl-icon-space` (`start`/`end`, bare for both).
