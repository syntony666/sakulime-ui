Add `sl-notice` — a notification capsule: in-flow it reads as a full-width banner, inside a consumer-owned fixed wrapper it reads as a toast. `sl-notice="fit"` shrink-wraps to content; `sl-notice-action` pushes trailing controls to the far edge. Consumes `sl-tone`, `sl-radius`, `sl-pad`; placement, stacking, dismiss, and timeout stay with the consumer.
